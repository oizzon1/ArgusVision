# IEEE TGRS — Author Facts (P4 primary, P2 fallback)

**Status: CONFIRMED from official GRSS page (fetched 2026-07-29).**

- **Format:** IEEE standard double-column, single-spaced; templates at template-selector.ieee.org (LaTeX/Word).
- **Length:** 10 printed pages base. Overlength Page Charge $230/page from page 11 (GRSS members $200/page; rates effective for submissions after 2026-01-01). Optional voluntary sustaining charge $110/page for the first 11.
- **Review:** single-blind, minimum two reviewers.
- **OA model:** hybrid — traditional (no fee) or Open Access APC **$2,800 (2026)**; IEEE member −5%, society member −20%.
- **Submission:** ieee.atyponrex.com/journal/tgrs only. ORCID mandatory for all authors.
- **Extras:** graphical abstract encouraged; author bios/photos customary; IEEE copyright transfer post-acceptance.

**Implications for P4:** ~12,000–15,000-word benchmark ≈ well over 10 IEEE pages — budget overlength charges or trim to ~13 pages; traditional (non-OA) route avoids the $2,800 APC.

Source: https://www.grss-ieee.org/publications/author-resources/tgrs-information-for-authors/ — retrieved 2026-07-29.
