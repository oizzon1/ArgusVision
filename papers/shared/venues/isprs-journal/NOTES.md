## Article types
- **Papers** (new research, technological developments or applications) — P2's
  type; **no stated word/page limit** (planned 9,000–10,000 words is within
  community norms).
- Review Papers; **Perspective Papers ≤4,000 words** (≤5 figures/tables, ≤50
  refs); Comments/Editorials.

## Confirmed mechanics
- **Citations: author–year** (Harvard-style; "et al." for 3+; groups
  alphabetical then chronological). Reference style applied by Elsevier at
  proof for accepted articles — don't over-invest in formatting pre-accept.
- **Review:** single anonymized; editor pre-screen (the high desk-reject bar
  the roadmap warns about); refereeing on a single PDF/Word file, free layout.
- Abstract + max **6 keywords**, American spelling, no general/plural terms.
- Graphical abstract / highlights: optional ("where applicable").
- Generalizability expectation appears in the guide (results should discuss
  limits of generalizability) — aligns with STRATEGIST's recorded concern
  about P2 having no cross-dataset test; the Limitations section must handle
  this head-on.
- Hybrid journal: subscription route free; OA option APC on separate page —
  **VERIFY amount only if OA is wanted** (subscription route is the default
  plan).
