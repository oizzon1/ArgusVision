## ⚠ Decisive finding: NO data-paper article type
Article types are exactly: **Papers** (new research, technological
developments or applications), **Review Papers**, and journal-column
contributions (letters/editorials). There is no descriptor/data-paper type.

**Consequence for P1:** the descriptor must be framed as a regular **Paper**
of the "technological development / application" flavor — dataset construction
methodology + validation as the research contribution, not a bare data
catalog. The Background→Methods→Records→Validation→Usage arc survives as
content, but Technical Validation carries the scientific weight (match-rate
analysis, per-class behavior, error taxonomy of the 2.1% non-matches).
Remaining check before committing: find 1–2 published dataset papers in this
journal as precedent (do at P1 outline time).

## Confirmed mechanics
- **Abstract ≤250 words**; 1–6 keywords (avoid multi-word keywords).
- **Review:** single anonymized, editor pre-screen, minimum two reviewers; one appeal allowed.
- **Format:** Word single-column or LaTeX (double-column allowed for LaTeX only); editable files required.
- **No stated word/page limit for Papers.** (~3,000-word P1 is comfortably fine.)
- **Datasets:** citing datasets in text + reference list explicitly encouraged; mark references with `[dataset]`; link the repository during submission. Our Zenodo DOI slots directly into this machinery.
- Preprints allowed (must be marked); standard Elsevier submission declaration.
- **APC:** journal is gold OA and an APC applies on acceptance; the amount is NOT in the guide (separate OA page) — **VERIFY amount + any ISPRS-member waiver before submission.**
